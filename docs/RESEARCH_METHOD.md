# Research method and reproducibility protocol

Each proposed ETPS system should include:
1. A concise research question.
2. Evidence label and justification.
3. Definitions and notation.
4. Assumptions, initial/boundary conditions, and domain of validity.
5. Derivation with cited primary or review literature.
6. A baseline calculation or counterexample.
7. A testable prediction where applicable.
8. Code, deterministic inputs, dependency versions, and tests.
9. Limitations and alternative interpretations.
10. A change log and author/date metadata.

## Evidence discipline
- Separate observation from interpretation.
- Prefer peer-reviewed papers, review articles, textbooks, and official experiment collaborations.
- Record DOI/arXiv identifiers when known; never fabricate citations.
- Distinguish analytic results from numerical approximations.
- State whether plots show illustrative equations, simulated data, or measurements.
