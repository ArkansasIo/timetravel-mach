# Reference starter list

This list contains established works useful for grounding the archive. Verify edition, page, and DOI metadata before using a reference in a formal publication.

1. Einstein, A. (1905). “On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies.” Annalen der Physik.
2. Einstein, A. (1916). “The Foundation of the General Theory of Relativity.” Annalen der Physik.
3. Misner, C. W., Thorne, K. S., & Wheeler, J. A. (1973). *Gravitation*. W. H. Freeman.
4. Wald, R. M. (1984). *General Relativity*. University of Chicago Press.
5. Carroll, S. M. (2004). *Spacetime and Geometry: An Introduction to General Relativity*. Addison-Wesley.
6. Taylor, E. F., & Wheeler, J. A. (1992). *Spacetime Physics* (2nd ed.). W. H. Freeman.
7. Hawking, S. W., & Ellis, G. F. R. (1973). *The Large Scale Structure of Space-Time*. Cambridge University Press.
8. Novikov, I. D. (1989). “Time machine and self-consistent evolution in problems with self-interacting particles.” Physical Review D, 45, 1989.
9. Morris, M. S., Thorne, K. S., & Yurtsever, U. (1988). “Wormholes, Time Machines, and the Weak Energy Condition.” Physical Review Letters, 61, 1446–1449.
10. Deutsch, D. (1991). “Quantum mechanics near closed timelike lines.” Physical Review D, 44, 3197.
11. Nielsen, M. A., & Chuang, I. L. (2010). *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information* (10th anniversary ed.). Cambridge University Press.
12. Breuer, H.-P., & Petruccione, F. (2002). *The Theory of Open Quantum Systems*. Oxford University Press.
13. Callen, H. B. (1985). *Thermodynamics and an Introduction to Thermostatistics* (2nd ed.). Wiley.
14. Planck Collaboration (2020). “Planck 2018 results. VI. Cosmological parameters.” Astronomy & Astrophysics, 641, A6.
