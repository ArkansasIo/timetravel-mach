# Temporal and multidimensional scenario catalog

These are research prompts and mathematical models, not confirmed technologies. Status labels indicate the strength of physical support, not the imaginative value of a scenario.

1. Relativistic clock aboard a fast spacecraft — ESTABLISHED; integrate proper time along a specified worldline.
2. Twin-paradox round trip — ESTABLISHED; piecewise inertial worldlines and acceleration transitions.
3. Gravitational clock offset — ESTABLISHED; compare idealized clocks in a specified metric.
4. GPS clock corrections — ESTABLISHED; combine special- and general-relativistic corrections in a system-specific model.
5. Black-hole exterior clock rate — ESTABLISHED within Schwarzschild assumptions; do not extrapolate across coordinate artifacts.
6. Cosmological expansion history — ESTABLISHED framework; parameter values depend on observations.
7. Entropy and the arrow of time — ACTIVE_RESEARCH; distinguish microscopic laws from low-entropy boundary conditions.
8. Quantum temporal correlations — ACTIVE_RESEARCH; correlations do not grant backward messaging.
9. Indefinite causal order — ACTIVE_RESEARCH; specify operational process formalism.
10. Closed timelike curves in selected metrics — ACTIVE_RESEARCH; mathematical existence does not establish physical realizability.
11. Traversable wormhole time shift — SPECULATIVE; requires unobserved objects and unresolved stability/energy questions.
12. Compact extra dimensions — SPECULATIVE; constrain with observations and collider/cosmological limits.
13. Landscape of vacuum states — SPECULATIVE; toy potential landscapes are not empirical maps of universes.
14. Quantum branching as alternate realities — INTERPRETATION-DEPENDENT; no assumption of controllable travel between branches.
15. Causal graph with a cycle — TOY_MODEL; graph consistency is an axiom or algorithmic property.
16. Entropy reversal in a bounded system — TOY_MODEL; local fluctuations do not reverse the universe's macroscopic arrow.
17. Mass-energy conversion thought experiment — ESTABLISHED relation; engineering efficiency and reaction channels are separate issues.
18. Uncertainty-limited clock — ESTABLISHED research area; distinguish quantum clock models from a universal time-energy rule.
19. Hawking radiation and black-hole thermodynamics — ACTIVE_RESEARCH; theoretical predictions and observational status must be separated.
20. Metric engineering / warp geometries — SPECULATIVE; solutions often require problematic stress-energy assumptions.
21. Causal-set discretization — ACTIVE_RESEARCH; candidate quantum-gravity framework, not established description of nature.
22. Holography and emergent spacetime — ACTIVE_RESEARCH; valid in specific theoretical contexts.
23. Quantum fields in curved spacetime — ESTABLISHED framework with limits; full quantum gravity remains unresolved.
24. Hypothetical inter-universe communication — FICTION unless a precise model and evidence are provided.
25. Timeline editing device — FICTION; no accepted physical mechanism.
