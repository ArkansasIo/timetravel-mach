# Scientific status and terminology

ETPS is a speculative research archive, not evidence that time travel or alternate realities are technologically achievable.

## Labels
- **ESTABLISHED:** supported by repeated observation and a mature theoretical framework in a stated domain.
- **ACTIVE_RESEARCH:** a legitimate research topic with unresolved questions.
- **TOY_MODEL:** an intentionally simplified mathematical construction; conclusions do not automatically transfer to nature.
- **SPECULATIVE:** plausible only under unconfirmed assumptions or lacking empirical confirmation.
- **FICTION:** a narrative premise.

## Important distinctions
1. Relativistic time dilation is established and measured. It does not permit arbitrary travel into the past.
2. General relativity admits unusual mathematical solutions, some containing closed timelike curves. Their physical realizability, stability, initial conditions, and quantum effects remain unresolved.
3. Traversable wormholes are hypothetical. No traversable wormhole has been observed; proposed time-shift mechanisms depend on assumptions not known to be physically realizable.
4. Extra dimensions occur in some theoretical frameworks but have not been experimentally established.
5. Quantum entanglement does not enable controllable faster-than-light or backward-in-time messaging.
6. The popular formula ΔE Δt ≳ ℏ/2 is not a universal energy-time version of the position-momentum uncertainty relation. The exact statement depends on the operational definition and physical setup.
7. “Quantum multiverse processor,” “temporal qubit,” and “history reconstruction” are not accepted technologies. If used, they must be identified as fiction or explicitly defined toy models.

## Research practice
Record assumptions, dimensions, domain of validity, boundary conditions, numerical method, uncertainty, and source provenance for each model. Never label a simulated graph as observational data.
